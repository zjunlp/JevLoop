any single dataset. This is a **connectivity and provenance audit**, not a measurement.

`--seed 20261002`, `--max-steps 4` (8 for environment datasets), `--max-tokens 2048`.
No paid API was called. Dirty workspace: none of these numbers are citable.

## Three findings worth keeping

**1. Two "failures" were correct refusals, not bugs.** `hotpotqa` and `triviaqa` refused the
`test` split with an explicit message: the official test annotations are withheld for the
leaderboard, use `--split validation`. Both run correctly on `validation`. Do not record
those as dataset defects.

**2. `bfcl-v3-live-simple` is available after all.** An earlier audit reported its data
files missing. The loader downloads via `hf_hub_download` on demand, and the sweep
triggered that download (`BFCL_v3_live_simple.json`, symlink mtime 19:23). The earlier
report described the pre-download state, not a defect.

**3. The QA-class datasets cannot test our claim.** `toy`, `gsm8k`, `strategyqa`,
`sportsunderstanding`, `physicsquestions`, `hotpotqa`, `triviaqa`, `fever`, `sotuqa` have no
tool use and no completion claim — they score final answers. They are in this sweep for
pipeline coverage only. A "0/3" or "1/3" here is a model-capability reading, not evidence
about control.

## Why this changes the plan

The sweep confirms the concern that started it: a single dataset would have misled us.
The datasets split into three different situations, and only group 1 can produce a native
number at all.

Recommended order from here:

1. **Group 1 only** for any method conclusion. Within it, `ternary-judge` has the native
   claim-level scorer and is the only one that scores *claims* rather than final answers.
2. Fix or explicitly scope **terminal-bench** to the 79 Dockerfile tasks before using it.
3. Wire official scorers for **BFCL** and **FTA** before their numbers count.
4. Leave **tau2** out until a user simulator exists.
